This Cookie Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies on Dot.Sheet.

## 1. What cookies we use

Dot.Sheet uses one essential cookie, `dot-sheet-session`, to keep you signed in and to protect your session against cross-site request forgery. This cookie is strictly necessary for Dot.Sheet to work — without it, you'd need to sign in again on every page.

We don't use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics cookies.

## 2. Why we use it

The `dot-sheet-session` cookie lets Dot.Sheet recognise you as you move between pages after signing in, and helps protect the authenticity of requests you submit (editing cells, sharing a spreadsheet, running an AI panel, etc.).

## 3. How long it lasts

The session cookie lasts for the duration of your browsing session, or longer if you select "Remember me" when signing in, per your browser's session-cookie handling.

## 4. Managing cookies

Because this cookie is essential to signing in and using Dot.Sheet, there's no in-app way to disable it while remaining signed in. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying signed in to Dot.Sheet.

## 5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
