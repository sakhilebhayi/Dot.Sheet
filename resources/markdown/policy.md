This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Sheet, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Sheet and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Sheet processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Spreadsheets and cell data** — the spreadsheets you own or are shared on, and the cell values, formulas, formatting, comments, and version history within them.

**Sharing and collaboration data** — who a spreadsheet is shared with, at what permission level (view, comment, edit, or admin), and any email-based invitations sent to collaborate on a spreadsheet, including to people who don't yet have a Dot Ecosystem account.

**AI panel activity** — if you use an AI-assisted panel (formula generation, natural-language query, data cleaning, OCR, or sentiment analysis), we log the request and response for reference. By default, this runs on our own local AI infrastructure; if a team configures an external provider (OpenAI or Anthropic), the relevant portion of your request is sent to that provider instead — see §3.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. AI processing and your cell data

Dot.Sheet's AI-assisted features run on a swappable provider. By default, this is a local AI model we run ourselves — no cell data leaves our own infrastructure. If a team has explicitly configured an external provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) for AI features, using an AI panel sends the relevant prompt and cell data to that provider to generate a response. We don't enable an external provider by default, and we don't use your cell data to train any AI model.

## 4. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- store, edit, and let you share and collaborate on spreadsheets;
- power AI-assisted panels when you choose to use them; and
- keep Dot.Sheet secure and prevent abuse.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Sheet (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Who can see your spreadsheets

A spreadsheet is visible to its owner, to your team if it's team-linked, and to anyone you've explicitly shared it with at a view, comment, edit, or admin permission level. If you create a public read-only link, anyone with that link can view the spreadsheet. We don't otherwise make your spreadsheets visible to other users or teams.

## 7. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and spreadsheet data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or a spreadsheet, the associated data is removed, except where we're required by law to retain certain records for longer.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, permission-tiered access control on every spreadsheet, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 10. Cookies

Dot.Sheet uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.
